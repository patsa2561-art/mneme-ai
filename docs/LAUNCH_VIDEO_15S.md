## Production notes

- **Music:** sparse synth pad, builds with each scene, drops in scene 4
- **Color palette:** dark (`#0a0a0e`) with orange→pink gradient (`#f38020`→`#ec4899`) accent on the JACKPOT card only
- **Captions:** burned-in EN; SRT for TH / JA / ZH / DE / ES
- **Logo:** μνήμη in lower-right corner from scene 2 onward, 30% opacity
- **No CTA card breaks** — CTA is final 2 seconds only

## Why this video works (short version)

In 15 seconds it shows:
1. The pain (generic AI suggestions are worthless)
2. The reveal (one personalised insight from YOUR repo)
3. The proof (it's different per repo, per day, per vendor — not a static template)
4. The action (`npx mneme jackpot` — instantly memorable)

That's the loop. No marketing fluff, no feature list, no logo soup.
