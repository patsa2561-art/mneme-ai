## 🎯 Why this matters

The bottleneck of every frontier-AI deployment in 2026 is **trust**, not capability. Safety-critical AI deployments — aerospace, autonomous systems, medical AI, financial AI compliance, regulated industries — all face the same wall:

- A hallucinated commit hash → a launch-window risk
- A vendor playing eval theater → a benchmark that misleads the procurement team
- A category-error paradox refuted as a falsehood → a logical contradiction in the audit chain
- A marketing claim that drifted from reality → a compliance failure

**Mneme is the only local-first, vendor-neutral truth layer that fixes all of these at the protocol level.** Every audit is HMAC-chained. Every claim is bound to a probe. Every vendor's vote is weighted by measured calibration on the user's own past work. Receipts can be re-verified offline weeks later by anyone.

This is the kind of infrastructure layer that lets a flight director, a CISO, a regulator, or a procurement lead say "yes, you can ship this AI to a safety-critical context — and here's the cryptographically-attested evidence."

## 🧭 How I work

- **Ship-first, audit-second.** Every release goes out only if PEAK GAUNTLET + TRUTH GATE both stay at 100/100. Mneme has shipped **37 versions** under this discipline.
- **Pinned regression tests over feature breadth.** Every bug becomes a test row encoded with `finding_id + broken_contract + fix_source_file + assertion`. Bug class extinction is measurable.
- **Honest receipts over marketing claims.** Mneme's own marketing copy is reconciled against measurable probes via TRUTH GATE (currently 16/16 pass). Drift becomes a CI failure, not a customer-discovered embarrassment.
- **Vendor-neutral by construction.** Mneme works the same way with Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen — by design, not by branding.
